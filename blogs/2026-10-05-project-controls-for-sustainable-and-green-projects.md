Monitoring and Reporting Green Project Progress Monitoring green project progress requires a systematic approach to evaluate and document sustainability metrics throughout the project lifecycle. Establishing clear benchmarks at the outset allows teams to track performance against predefined environmental goals. Regular assessments facilitate a proactive stance in addressing any discrepancies between expected and actual outcomes.
