Performance Measurement and Reporting Effective performance measurement serves as a cornerstone for successful project controls. By establishing clear metrics tied directly to organizational objectives, teams can better understand their project’s progress and identify areas needing improvement. These metrics should be quantifiable, enabling easy tracking and reporting throughout the project lifecycle.
