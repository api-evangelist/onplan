Using Historical Data for Cost Baselines Historical data serves as a critical resource in developing cost baselines for projects. By analyzing costs from previous endeavors, project managers can uncover trends and patterns that inform future budgeting. Past projects provide valuable insights into both direct expenditures and indirect costs, helping to establish realistic financial expectations.
