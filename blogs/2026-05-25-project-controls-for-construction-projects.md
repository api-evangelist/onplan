The Role of Technology in Project Controls The integration of technology in project controls revolutionizes how construction projects are managed. Advanced software solutions streamline processes such as scheduling, budgeting, and risk management, enhancing overall efficiency. Real-time data analytics allow project managers to monitor progress closely, enabling informed decision-making that can adapt to project changes swiftly.
