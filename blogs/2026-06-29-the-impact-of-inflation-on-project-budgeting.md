Inflation’s Impact on Resource Allocation Resource allocation becomes increasingly challenging in a climate of rising costs. Teams often find that their original budgets no longer reflect the true expense of materials and labor. As prices surge, essential resources may become scarce, forcing project managers to make difficult choices.
