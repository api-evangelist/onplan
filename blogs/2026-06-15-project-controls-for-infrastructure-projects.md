Risk Management in Infrastructure Projects Effective risk management is essential in the successful execution of infrastructure projects. Identifying potential risks early in the project lifecycle allows teams to assess their impact and likelihood, facilitating proactive measures. This approach minimizes disruptions during construction and ensures that resources are allocated efficiently.
