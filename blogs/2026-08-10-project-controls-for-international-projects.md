Communication in Project Controls Effective communication serves as the backbone of successful project controls in an international context. Diverse teams often face challenges related to language barriers and differing cultural norms. Immediate and clear communication fosters collaboration, ensuring that all stakeholders are aligned on objectives and expectations.
